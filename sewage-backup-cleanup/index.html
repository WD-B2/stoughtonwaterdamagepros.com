Why Is My Basement Drain Gurgling or Overflowing After Rain?

A basement floor drain that gurgles, a toilet that bubbles when the washer drains, or water rising in a utility sink after a storm all point to sewage backup cleanup in Stoughton. Call our 24/7 line. Keep everyone out of the room, do not flush or run water, and do not try to clean it with a household mop.

Sewage water carries bacteria and viruses, so the first rule is to limit contact.

Treat every backup as contaminated

Even a small amount of sewage water can leave harmful organisms on floors and in porous materials. Children, pets and anyone with health concerns should stay away until cleanup is done.

Until the crew arrives:

  • Stop using water fixtures in the house
  • Keep people and pets out of the affected area
  • Do not use fans, which can spread airborne contamination
  • Photograph the backup for your records

Why sewer lines back up

Backups come from three places. A clog in your own lateral line affects one home. A blockage or surcharge in the municipal main can affect several homes at once, and heavy rain can overload systems. Roots, grease and wipes are common blockage causes.

Ask the Stoughton DPW about reported issues in your neighborhood, since we do not speak for the town's sewer system.

How to tell it is sewage

Look and smell for these.

Foul odor

Sewage gas has a distinct smell that does not clear with ventilation.

Slow or gurgling drains

Several fixtures acting up at once suggest a main line problem.

Water in the lowest drain

Floor drains and tubs in basements fill first.

Dark or cloudy water

Discoloration and debris confirm the source.

How we clean up a sewage backup

The sequence protects both your health and our crew.

  1. Contain and protect

    The crew sets up protective gear and isolates the area.

  2. Remove contaminated water

    Pumps and extractors take out the liquid.

  3. Remove porous materials

    Carpet, pad and soaked drywall are removed and bagged.

  4. Clean, disinfect and dry

    Surfaces are cleaned and treated, then dried with equipment.

Salvage rules after sewage
