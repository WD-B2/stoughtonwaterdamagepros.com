Homes Around Ames Long Pond

Ames Long Pond is an 86 acre pond that borders the Stoughton Memorial Conservation Land, according to a Homes.com guide to the town. Real estate listings label nearby streets as Ames Long Pond and South Stoughton Ames Pond, and a 55 plus community called Village at Ames Pond lies along the Stoughton and Easton line.

No source we found sets a boundary, so we describe the area by its streets and call it approximate.

A Documented Repeat Flooding Spot

The 2024 Abington, Easton and Stoughton Hazard Mitigation Plan lists a culvert on West Street at Ames Long Pond, between Highland Street and Lake Drive, among the town's known repeated flooding areas. It is one of five such locations in the plan.

That is a road and culvert location. It does not mean every nearby home floods. Neighborhood-specific FEMA flood-zone status not verified. Check the FEMA Flood Map Service Center for your address, and remember that a low-risk zone does not mean flooding cannot happen.

What Culvert Flooding Means for a Homeowner

Road and culvert flooding can block access, and runoff can push water toward low yards, garages and basements. The first response is to stay out of moving or deep water on the road.

If water has entered the house, our storm water damage cleanup and basement water damage cleanup pages describe the steps.

Seasonal and Waterfront Homes

Pond homes often have finished lower levels, garages and decks near the water. A burst pipe in a seasonal house can run unnoticed, so a frozen pipe problem may only show up when someone returns.

If you find water after time away, shut off the main only if the floor is dry, then call.

Safety Before Cleanup

Avoid floodwater

Do not drive or walk through water on a road, since depth and current are hard to judge.

Watch the electrics

Never operate switches or equipment while standing in water.

Protect what is dry

Raise furniture and boxes and move vehicles to higher ground if it is safe.

Take photos

Record the water level, the walls and the damaged items before moving them.

Extraction and Drying Near the Pond

Pumps and extractors remove standing water, and the crew checks whether it came from clean supply lines or from outside. Outside water is handled as contaminated.

After removal, drying equipment runs until moisture readings are normal. Our water extraction page explains how the first visit works.

Insurance Notes

Standard homeowners policies usually exclude flood and seepage. A separate flood policy is the usual route, so ask your insurer before you need it.

Photos, readings and a written scope help describe the work. Coverage depends on the cause and the policy.
