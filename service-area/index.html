How to Tell Which Page Fits Your Address

What Happens After You Call From Any Town

The dispatcher takes the address and the details of the water. A crew leaves Stoughton with pumps, extractors and drying equipment. On arrival, the lead explains the plan and the price, then work starts. Readings are taken until the structure is dry.

That sequence is the same whether the job is in Stoughton, Avon, Holbrook, Brockton or East Walpole.

Planning Ahead Wherever You Live

Whether you live in Stoughton or a neighboring town, three habits reduce water damage. Know where your main shutoff is. Check your insurance for flood, sewer backup and sump pump coverage. Keep our number and your insurer's claim line where you can find them quickly.

A little preparation turns a panicked call into a calm one.

Questions Neighbors Ask Most

People ask whether we charge extra for distance, whether we cover weekends and holidays and whether we work with their insurer. Scope and price are explained before work starts in every town. The line is open every day of the year. We provide photos, readings and a written scope that you can hand to your insurer, who decides coverage.
