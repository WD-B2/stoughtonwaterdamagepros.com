Wall and drywall water damage repair in Stoughton begins with finding how far the moisture goes. Call us to plan a visit. Turn off the water source, remove baseboards if you can do it safely, and keep furniture away from the wall. Do not paint over stains, which traps moisture.

Drywall is mostly paper and gypsum, and both hold water.

How walls get wet

Plumbing in walls, roof leaks, window leaks, and flooding from below all feed moisture into drywall. Water wicks upward in drywall about as far as it is wet at the base.

Insulation in the cavity can hold water against framing even after the drywall surface looks dry.

Signs of wet drywall

Check the lower wall and corners first.

Stains or tide marks

Rings show repeated wetting.

Soft or crumbling drywall

Pressing gently reveals soft areas.

Peeling paint or tape

Moisture breaks the bond.

Musty smell near baseboard

Odor often precedes visible mold.

How we repair wet walls

Open only what needs to be opened.

  1. Measure the cavity

    Meters and thermal imaging show where moisture is.

  2. Remove what cannot be dried

    Saturated drywall and insulation are cut out.

  3. Dry framing and cavity

    Air movers and dehumidifiers dry the space.

  4. Prepare for repair

    Edges are cut clean for patching.

What to Expect During Wall Work

The crew starts with readings at several heights, because moisture wicks upward and the visible stain is only part of the story. A thermal camera helps find cold, wet spots behind the paint. You are shown the readings and the proposed cuts before any drywall is removed.

Cuts are made in straight lines about a foot above the highest reading, so patching is simpler later. Wet insulation is pulled and bagged, and the stud bay is left open to dry.

Mistakes That Hide Wall Problems

Painting over a stain is the most common one, followed by caulking a gap and calling it fixed. Stain-blocking paint can seal moisture into the wall, where it keeps feeding mold. Skipping the insulation is another trap, since wet insulation holds water against studs for weeks.

Closing the wall too soon is the third. Framing should read dry before new drywall goes up.

  • Do not paint a wall until readings are normal
  • Replace wet insulation, not just the drywall
  • Keep the removed drywall pieces for your adjuster if asked

After the Wall Is Dry

When framing reads dry, a contractor can patch drywall, prime and paint. Ask for mold-resistant board in damp areas. Fix the original source first, or the new wall will face the same problem.

Standards and Sources Behind This Page

The ANSI/IICRC S500 standard for professional water damage restoration describes how to decide between drying drywall in place and removing it, based on the water category and how long materials stayed wet. The EPA guide to mold and moisture adds that wet building materials should be dried within a day or two to prevent mold.
