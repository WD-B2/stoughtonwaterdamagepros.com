A pipe that is frozen but not yet leaking is a warning, not a clean bill of health.

What to Do Before the Pipe Thaws

Ice expands inside the pipe and splits the wall, but the ice plug also seals the split. When it melts, pressurized water pours through the crack. A house that looked fine at 6 a.m. can be flooding by noon.

If you suspect a frozen line:

  • Close the main shutoff before the pipe thaws
  • Open the faucet nearest the frozen section
  • Never use an open flame to thaw a pipe
  • Call us at the first sign of running water inside a wall

Cold weather patterns that hit Stoughton

The January 2015 and January 2022 winter storms were both federally declared disasters in Massachusetts. Long stretches of sub-freezing nights are what cause the trouble, especially in pipes that run along exterior walls, through garages or above unheated crawl spaces.

Vacant homes and seasonal use make the problem worse, since nobody notices a leak right away.

Early warnings before a pipe cracks

Catch these and you may avoid a flood.

No water from a faucet

A tap that runs dry in cold weather often means a frozen line behind it.

Frost on exposed pipe

Visible frost shows the pipe is cold enough to freeze solid.

Bulging or cracked pipe

A visible bulge means the pipe is already stressed.

Smell from a drain

A frozen drain line can block flow and send odors indoors.

How we handle the cleanup

The sequence keeps plumbing repair and restoration from getting in each other's way.

  1. Locate the water

    Meters and a thermal camera find where water ran beyond the visible stain.

  2. Remove water

    Pumps and extractors clear the floor, and wet insulation is bagged.

  3. Dry the cavity

    Dehumidification runs until readings come down to a normal level.

  4. Report the loss

    We prepare moisture readings and photos for your insurance claim.
