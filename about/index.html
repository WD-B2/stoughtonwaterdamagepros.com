Who We Are

Stoughton Water Damage Pros is a water damage restoration service based at 3 Hawes Way in Stoughton, Massachusetts. We handle emergency water removal, structural drying, cleanup after pipe, appliance and flood events, and mold remediation tied to water damage.

We serve Stoughton, Avon, Holbrook, Brockton and East Walpole. The business is built around one phone line that is staffed at every hour, because water damage rarely happens on a convenient schedule.

How We Work

Every job follows the same order. Stop the source, remove the water, dry the structure, document the loss. The crew explains each step before it starts, and the price is explained before work begins. We do not quote an exact number over the phone without knowing the situation, because water damage is rarely the same twice.

What Happens on a Typical Call

A typical call starts with someone phoning about water on a kitchen floor. The dispatcher asks where the water is coming from, how much there is and whether anyone is in danger. If the source is still running, the first instruction is to close a valve. Then the dispatcher assigns a crew and gives an honest estimate of when it can arrive.

On site, the crew lead checks the source, reads the moisture and explains the plan. Water is extracted, wet materials are removed if needed and drying equipment is set. Daily readings follow until the structure is dry, and the paperwork is handed over at the end.

What We Believe About Doing This Work

Water damage is stressful, and the people we work for are often scared. We think the right response is plain talk, a clear plan and a price they hear before work begins. We do not use scare tactics, and we do not promise things we cannot control, like arrival times in a storm.

We also believe in telling you when a job is not ours. Roof leaks need roofers, bad drainage needs a drainage contractor and a failed pipe needs a plumber. We handle the water and the drying, and we say where others come in.

Our Service Standards

Every job includes moisture readings at the start and during drying, photos before and after, a written scope you can show an insurer and a plain explanation of what we found. We aim to leave your home safe, dry and ready for repair.

  • Scope and price explained before work starts
  • Daily readings during drying
  • Photos and logs for your insurer
  • Honest advice on what belongs to other trades

What We Do Not Do

We do not repair roofs, replace plumbing or install foundation drainage. Those are other trades, and we say so. We also do not decide insurance coverage. Your insurer does, and we try to give you the documentation to support your claim.

Restoration is not waterproofing. If water keeps coming back, you may need a drainage or foundation contractor in addition to us.
