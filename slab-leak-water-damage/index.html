Why Is Part of My Floor Warm or My Water Bill Rising?

A warm patch on the floor, the sound of running water when everything is off, or a water bill that keeps climbing can all point to slab leak water damage in Stoughton. Call our 24/7 line if you see water coming up through the floor. Shut off the main supply to stop the leak, and keep your photos and your last two water bills handy.

A slab leak is a pipe failure under or inside a concrete floor, and it is a plumbing job first.

Water under the slab does not stay under it

It finds cracks, expansion joints and the edges of the slab, then rises into carpet, wood and baseboard. Because it starts below the floor, damage can be well along before you see anything.

If you suspect a slab leak:

  • Close the main shutoff and see if the meter still moves
  • Check floors for warm spots, dampness or lifted edges
  • Call a plumber or leak detection company for the pipe
  • Call us for water removal and drying

What causes leaks beneath the floor

Copper lines can pit and corrode, plastic lines can shift as the ground settles, and heating pipes cast in a slab can fail. Homes with a full slab foundation, a slab addition or radiant heating pipes cast in concrete are the usual candidates.

The soil type and nearby groundwater also affect how fast a small leak spreads.

Common signs of a slab leak

Any two together are worth a plumber's visit.

Warm floor spots

Hot water leaking beneath the slab warms the surface.

Unexplained water bill increase

A steady rise with no change in use is a classic clue.

Sound of running water

A hiss or rush with all fixtures off suggests a pressurized leak.

Cracks or damp patches

New cracks along baseboard or damp carpet edges are worth checking.

What we do for the water damage

We handle the building side while your plumber handles the pipe.

  1. Map moisture

    Meters and a thermal camera map where water has spread.

  2. Remove water and wet materials

    Wet carpet, pad and baseboard are pulled where needed.

  3. Dry floors and walls

    Mats and dehumidifiers dry the slab surface and wall base.

  4. Verify and document

    Readings and photos are recorded for your claim.

What affects cost

Cleanup price follows the size of the wet area, flooring type and drying time. The plumbing repair is billed separately by the plumber. We explain scope and price before starting.

Insurance notes:

  • Many policies cover resulting water damage but not the pipe itself
  • Some policies exclude the cost of reaching the pipe through the slab
  • Gradual leaks are often excluded, so act as soon as you suspect one

Ask your insurer how it treats slab access costs.
