Burst pipes are emergencies even when the leak seems small

A pinhole spray inside a wall can run for hours before a stain appears. By then insulation is saturated and drywall is soft. Treat any unexplained sound of running water as urgent.

What to do right away:

  • Find and close the main water shutoff, usually near the meter
  • Drain remaining water by running the lowest faucet
  • Photograph the pipe and every wet surface
  • Call a licensed plumber for the repair and call us for the water

Why pipes fail in Stoughton homes

New England winters set the pattern. Massachusetts has seen repeated winter storm disaster declarations, including DR-4214 in January 2015 and DR-4651 in January 2022, and each cold snap stresses exposed supply lines in garages, attics and exterior walls.

Not every burst is cold related. Old fittings, high water pressure and corrosion at joints cause failures in mild weather too.

How to tell a pipe has burst

Some failures are loud. Many are quiet.

A sudden drop in pressure

Faucets that sputter or slow can mean a break upstream.

Sounds inside walls

Hissing or dripping behind a wall points to a pressurized leak.

Ceiling stains or bubbling paint

Water travels along framing before it shows, so the stain is rarely above the break.

A water bill that jumps

A usage spike with no change in routine can signal an unseen break.

What happens after you call

Each step is explained before it starts.

  1. Confirm the source is off

    The crew checks that supply is closed and the break is isolated.

  2. Extract and open up

    Standing water is removed, and wet baseboard or drywall is cut back only as far as needed.

  3. Dry the cavity

    Air movers and dehumidifiers run until meter readings match dry reference materials.

  4. Document for your claim

    Photos, moisture logs and a written scope go to you for your insurer.

What affects the price of a burst pipe job

The main factors are how long the water ran, how many rooms it reached, whether it dropped through a ceiling into a lower floor, and how many days of drying equipment are needed. We give you the scope and the price before work starts.

General insurance points:

  • Massachusetts policies commonly cover sudden and accidental pipe breaks
  • Coverage can depend on the home being heated and occupied
  • Gradual leaks often are not covered, so report the loss promptly and document the date

The cost of the pipe repair itself is separate and usually handled by your plumber.
